How it works

  • Around 50 South African sources are checked continuously: government, Parliament (PMG), the police service, courts, municipalities and newsrooms.
  • Each story is sorted by topic and place, and ranked by how many people it affects.
  • A person approves what is published. Nothing goes out unreviewed.
  • You get a headline, a short summary and a link to the original publisher. We never copy articles.
  • Mistakes are corrected publicly.
