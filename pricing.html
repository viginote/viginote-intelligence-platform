Common questions
How quickly can I receive a brief?
Standard turnaround is 24-48 hours from confirmed scope. For urgent situations — active crises, imminent travel — contact info@viginote.com directly and we will advise on expedited options.
What does the revision include?
Every brief includes one revision. This covers additions, reframing for a different audience, updating with new intelligence, or adjusting the analytical emphasis. It does not cover a complete change of subject.
Can I share the brief with colleagues or clients?
Yes. Every brief is delivered with a secure shareable link at intel.viginote.com/view/{token}. Anyone with the link can view the brief — no login required. Links are valid for 30 days.
What sources does Viginote use?
Viginote monitors 162+ verified open-source feeds across 7 global regions — wire services, regional outlets, local sources, maritime databases, government statements and specialist OSINT publications. All sources are tier-graded and corroborated before appearing in a brief.
Is there a minimum commitment for subscriptions?
No minimum commitment for Analyst and Operator tiers. Enterprise is by annual arrangement. All subscriptions are by enquiry only — contact info@viginote.com to discuss.
Can I upgrade from a one-off brief to a subscription?
Yes — and this is the most common path. Commission a brief, see the product, then contact us to set up ongoing access. We'll apply the brief cost as credit toward your first month.
