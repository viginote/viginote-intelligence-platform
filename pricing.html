Common questions

Is there a free trial?
No. Viginote is a professional intelligence service. The free Monitor tier gives you access to our public Telegram channel so you can assess the quality of our intelligence before committing to a subscription. For paid tiers, contact us at info@viginote.com to discuss your requirements.
How do I get access to the platform?
All paid subscriptions are set up manually. Email info@viginote.com with your tier of interest and we'll onboard you within 24 hours. Your credentials are sent securely and you access the platform via viginote.com.
What regions and streams are available?
Regions: Middle East, Europe, Asia, West/East Africa, Southern Africa, South America, and Global. Streams: Geographic (conflict & security), Maritime, Cyber, Economic, Political, Executive Protection. Your tier determines how many of each you can access.
What is a watchlist alert?
You define up to 50 terms — country names, actor names, organisations, topics. Viginote monitors every incoming alert for these terms. When a match scores above 8/10 severity, you receive an immediate push notification. This is the core duty-of-care feature for field security teams.
What are the contract terms?
Analyst and Operator: monthly, no minimum term. Enterprise: monthly or annual. Bespoke: 3-month minimum, negotiated individually. Annual subscriptions save 17%.
Who is Viginote built for?
NGO field security managers, corporate security directors, risk consultants, private equity analysts with emerging market exposure, executive protection firms, maritime operators, and legal/insurance teams requiring country risk intelligence.
